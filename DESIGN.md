# Design System: Vizier

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Departures Board"**

Every dictation is a departure. When Right Command goes down, the board posts the destination, the words settle like flaps coming to rest, and the board logs how each take arrived. Vizier is drawn as a split-flap board: matte black modules with a hairline split, enamel-white condensed characters, tabular figures, and one reserved color. Amber means the mic is live and appears nowhere else.

Two densities share one material. The recording indicator is a single low row floating over whatever app has focus, often a terminal in full screen: a lamp, the posted destination, the arriving words, and a status flap. The arriving words are the only large thing on it. The deliberate surfaces (the popover, History, Re-run, Settings, Permissions) are dense timetables built from the same modules and the same label grid, so every row reads like a line on the board: time, destination, platform, words, status. The printed timetable is the config, and the mode editor is a line diagram.

Gradients paint only the hairline split across every flap, the lamp's glass, and the fade where an overflowing word row runs off its left edge. The only light is the lamp, whose brightness comes from the real audio buffer. Surfaces are opaque; nothing is frosted, blurred, or lit for decoration. The world refuses the frosted bottom pill with a gradient waveform.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Matte, opaque black surfaces; every module, plate and control is a flap split by a 1px hairline at its midline.
- One variable grotesque, Archivo, set across its width axis from 62 (wordmark and window titles) to 100 (text you read).
- Hierarchy by scale: the arriving words are 24px on the indicator, and nothing else there is larger than 13.5px.
- Outcome read from the plate's fill: PASTED plain, RE-ROUTED slate, HELD white, FAILED red, CANCELLED gray.
- Short mechanical motion (70 to 200 ms) that follows real changes and lands instantly under Reduce Motion.
- Uppercase for labels, plates, controls and the destination; dictated words stay in the case they paste in.

## Colors

Enamel on black, five plate tones for outcomes, and one reserved amber for the live mic.

### Primary
- **Mic-Live Amber** (#ffb000): the lit lamp on the recording indicator and the lamp dot in the menu bar status item, and nothing else. It lights only while real samples are flowing. The indicator lamp is a radial fill from **Lamp Hot Spot** (#ffe3a3) at 42% 38% through Mic-Live Amber at 55% to **Lamp Rim** (#c98500).
- **Unlit Lamp Glass** (#1c1812): the same lamp when the mic is not live, a radial fill from **Unlit Glass Highlight** (#2b261c) through Unlit Lamp Glass at 60% to **Unlit Glass Rim** (#0e0c08). It is the dark glass of the one lamp, not a second use of amber.

### Secondary
- **Re-routed Slate** (#5b6b7a): the hue of every "took another route" signal. As a plate it is **Slate Flap** (#62727f over #56656f, split #4c5a66, ink #f3f1ec); as a wash it tints the CHANGED ON DISK banner.

### Tertiary
- **Failed Red** (#c8102e): failure and lost meaning. As a plate it is **Red Flap** (#cf1432 over #bd0f2b, split #a30d26, ink #ffffff). The base red draws the critical stop ring in Re-run, the 2px strike and underline on a critical diff, the error line in the file gutter, and the status item's alert square. **Signal Red Text** (#ff6b7f) carries a "Dropped ..." line and a critical stage's change count on black; **Struck Red** (#ff8b9b) is the struck-out words of a critical drop.

### Neutral
- **Board Black** (#141414): the indicator strip, the remarks line, the cancel residue, the vocabulary cells, and the ink on white plates.
- **Window Black** (#121212): popover and window grounds.

### Plate Tones
Each plate is a top face, a bottom face, a 1px split and an ink.
- **Plain** (Flap Top, Flap Bottom, Plate Split, Enamel White): PASTED, GRANTED, RUNNING, CHECKING, DELAYED.
- **Slate** (Slate Flap): RE-ROUTED, BATCH, CONNECTING, NO LIVE TEXT, PROBE, CHANGED ON DISK.
- **White** (Held White): HELD, NO SIGNAL, RELAUNCH.
- **Red** (Red Flap): FAILED, DENIED, NOT GRANTED, MEANING CHANGED, BROKEN FILE.
- **Gray** (Cancelled Gray): CANCELLED.

### Increase Contrast
Under Increase Contrast, Rule becomes #4a4a4a, Rule Two #6a6a6a, Faded Enamel #cfcbc2, Dim Enamel #aaa59c, and the Turning Tail rgb(234 231 224 / 0.78). The strip, the remarks line and windows swap their faint outline for 1px #8a8680, and unselected flap controls gain a 1px inset #6a6a6a so they read as controls.

### Named Rules
**The Reserved Amber Rule.** Amber (#ffb000) means the mic is live and appears nowhere else: not on focus, selection, hover, a badge, a button, the wordmark or a chart. If amber is showing, real samples are flowing.

**The Tone-by-Fill Rule.** An outcome is read from the plate's whole fill, never from a colored edge, stripe or dot beside neutral content. A wider warning (a meaning change, a broken file, a file changed on disk) takes a faint wash of its hue with a 1px inset outline and holds a plate that names the problem.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo, variable (width 62 to 125, weight 100 to 900), with system-ui fallback
**Body Font:** Archivo at width 100
**Label/Mono Font:** Archivo condensed (width 70 to 75) for labels and plates; JetBrains Mono for the config file view only

**Character:** One grotesque stretched along its width axis does the work of a whole board: tight and heavy for plates, labels and the wordmark, near-normal for the words you read. Every number uses tabular figures.

The Swift build sets both axes through font variations: `wdth` is the CSS font-stretch percentage below, and `wght` the weight. Letter-spacing is in em; multiply by the point size for tracking. The app bundles Archivo (OFL) with both axes intact (`Resources/Fonts/`).

### Hierarchy
- **Word** (560, 24px, 36px line, width 80, 0.005em): the arriving words on the indicator's word plates, in mixed case.
- **Clock** (800, 31px on 40px modules, width 72): the posted time at the head of History's detail.
- **Mark** (800, 19px, width 62, 0.16em): VIZIER in the popover head.
- **Window Title** (800, 17px, width 62, 0.14em, uppercase): HISTORY, RE-RUN, SETTINGS, PERMISSIONS.
- **Reading** (400, 15px, 1.5): pasted text and verbatim speech, 60ch in History and 80ch in Re-run.
- **Title** (700, 15px, 1.2): a mode's name in the timetable.
- **Field Value** (600, 14px, width 87, tabular): the values under History's detail labels.
- **Diff** (400, 14px, 1.75): the Re-run word diff; the tall line leaves room for strikes, underlines and stage tags.
- **Remarks** (500, 13.5px, 1.35): the reason line beside the strip.
- **Body** (400, 13px, 1.4): default popover and window text.
- **Status Plate** (800, 13px on a 24px plate, width 70, 0.08em): the strip's status flap.
- **Row** (500, 12.5px, width 87, tabular): board rows in the popover and History; the row's text column returns to width 100 at weight 400.
- **Code Cell** (700, 12px, width 72, 0.05em): two-letter platform codes (GS, SQ, GL).
- **Control** (700, 11.5px, width 72, 0.08em, uppercase): flap buttons, segments, doors, the search placeholder. Keycaps use the same size at width 75 and 0.04em.
- **Plate** (800, 11px, width 70, 0.08em) and **Plate Small** (800, 9.5px, width 70, 0.07em): status plates in windows and in rows.
- **Module** (700, 10.5px in a 10×15px cell, width 72): the destination, timer and platform code on the strip. The popover clock sets 13px in 13×19px cells.
- **Label** (700, 10px, 1.2, width 75, 0.12em, uppercase, Dim Enamel): the label grid.
- **Status Sub** (700, 9.5px, 11px line, width 75, 0.12em, tabular, Dim Enamel): the line under the strip's status plate (+1.0 S, VIA BATCH).
- **Tag** (700, 8.5px, 13px line, width 75, 0.06em): stage tags TR, CL, RP.
- **Code** (JetBrains Mono 400, 12px, 19px line): the config file editor, its gutter, and the file path.

### Named Rules
**The Only Large Thing Rule.** On the indicator the arriving words (24px) are the only large type. Destination, timer, platform, status and remarks stay at 13.5px or smaller so the eye goes to the words.

**The One Label Grid Rule.** Every column head and field label on every surface uses the Label style and one vocabulary: TIME, TO, PLAT, WORDS, STATUS, with LENGTH, AFTER STOP, VIA and AUDIO in History's detail. A mode is a platform, shown as its two-letter code.

**The What Pastes Rule.** Dictated words are set in the case they will paste in. Uppercase belongs to labels, plates, controls and the destination modules, never to the words.

## Layout

Vizier's surfaces are the recording indicator (a floating, non-activating panel that never takes focus and ignores the pointer except on the cancel residue), the menu bar status item, the popover under it, and four ordinary windows: History, Re-run, Settings and Permissions.

**The strip.** One row, 62px tall and as wide as min(880px, display width less 96px), padded 0 12px 0 14px. Four columns with a 14px gutter: the lamp (20px), the meta block, the word row (takes the rest), and the status column (at least 112px, right-aligned). The meta block stacks, 5px apart, a destination row (an 11px arrow icon, 5px gap, 8 character modules) over a sub row indented 16px (a 5-module timer, 8px gap, a 2-module platform code). The word row is 40px tall and holds 36px plates 4px apart. The status column stacks its plate over the sub line, 3px apart.

**Placement.** Low and centered on the display with the cursor. Exact placement remains an open product decision, so it has no token.

**The Fixed Strip Rule.** The strip is the fixed point. The remarks line grows away from it, above when the strip sits low and below when it sits high, and nothing ever pushes the strip.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is material, not atmosphere. Surfaces are opaque fills; a flap's two tones and its hairline split give it the only relief it needs. Things that float over other apps (the strip, the remarks line, the residue, the popover) carry a 1px outline and a soft drop shadow; ordinary windows take the system window shadow and a 1px Outline. There is no blur, vibrancy or translucent material anywhere, so Reduce Transparency has nothing to remove and the world ships no variant for it. The few alpha values (the Turning Tail ink, warning washes, the left-edge fade on an overflowing word row) always sit over an opaque ground.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Strip** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgb(255 255 255 / 0.06), inset 0 1px 0 rgb(255 255 255 / 0.04), 0 14px 36px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.55), 0 3px 8px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.4)`): the recording strip over any app.
- **Remarks** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgb(255 255 255 / 0.06), 0 8px 22px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.45)`): the remarks line.
- **Residue** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 22px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.45)` with a 1px Rule Two border): the cancel residue.
- **Popover** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #2a2a2a, 0 22px 60px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.6), 0 4px 12px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.35)`): the popover.
- **Window Edge** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #2a2a2a`): windows, under the system shadow.
- **Lamp Bezel** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.6), 0 0 0 3px #0d0d0d`): the lamp's socket.
- **Lamp Glow** (`box-shadow: 0 0 calc(4px + level × 26px) calc(level × 3px) rgb(255 176 0 / calc(0.3 + level × 0.55))`): the live lamp only, recomputed from the buffer level every frame. It washes over the socket and falls on the board under the plates, and it stops at the board's edge. The lamp is deliberately bright, because early use showed a dim one was easy to miss.
- **Contrast Edge** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #8a8680`): replaces the faint outline on the strip, remarks line and windows under Increase Contrast.

### Named Rules
**The Matte Board Rule.** Every surface is an opaque fill. No frosted material, blur, vibrancy or static glow; the only light in the system is the live lamp.

## Shapes

Small, mechanical radii taken from real flap hardware: 2px on character modules, platform codes, flap controls, fields and stage tags; 3px on word plates, status plates, keycaps and the waveform well; 6px on notes and popover mode rows; 7px on banners; 8px on the cancel residue and the file editor; 10px on the strip and remarks line; 12px on the popover. Circles appear only for the lamp and the stops of the line diagram.

Every flap is painted top face to 50% − 0.5px, a 1px split, bottom face from 50% + 0.5px. The line diagram is a 2px Line Track with 12px stops drawn as 2px Enamel White rings on Window Black. In the timetable the two ends (Microphone and Paste) are filled 12px squares with 2px corners; in Re-run the origin is a filled circle, a selected stage fills and gains a 3px Window Black gap and 1px Enamel White ring, and a stage that lost meaning takes a Failed Red ring. A route's fallback hangs below it after a 1px dashed rule (#3d3c38).

Icons are authored for the world: 16px grid, 1.5px stroke, round caps and joins, current color (arrow, play, stop, copy, re-run, close, search, plus, open, check).

### Named Rules
**The Hairline Split Rule.** Every flap surface (character modules, word plates, status plates, platform codes, keycaps, flap buttons, segments, doors) is two tones split by a 1px hairline at its exact midline. This split is the world's material; a flap without it belongs to another world.

## Components

### Recording Indicator (the strip)
The signature: a one-row departures board that posts where the words will land, lets them arrive, and names the outcome.
- **Anatomy:** lamp; destination row "→ NOTES" in 8 modules; timer in 5 modules showing captured audio (0:05); platform code in 2 modules; the word row; the status flap and its sub line.
- **Arming and recording:** the strip appears whole in one frame at key-down with the lamp dark. One frame later the destination modules flip to the focused app (VIZIER when Vizier itself has focus). The lamp lights when real samples arrive: opacity 0.55 + 0.45 × level, with the Lamp Glow. Level is the buffer's mean square mapped from −62 dB (dark) to −22 dB (full), rising with a 24 ms time constant and falling with 150 ms. Full sits at −22 dB because real dictation ran at a median near −31 dB, and the lab's −10 dB left the lamp short of full on every take. The timer flips each second of captured audio. The status flap stays empty while things are normal.
- **Live words:** settled words are solid plates; interim words are Tail plates (Turning Tail ink on the darker Tail Flap). A word flips only when its text changes. When a final lands, removed fillers and self-corrections collapse out of the line. When words overflow, the line slides left and its left edge fades from transparent at 10px to solid at 48px; the row keeps at most 64 plates and trims to the newest 40.
- **Trouble while recording:** a connection still opening after 1.2 s shows CONNECTING (slate) over RECORDING. A dropped stream shows NO LIVE TEXT (slate) over STILL RECORDING and dims the frozen words to 55%. A dead mic (no voice and more than 2.5 s of silence) shows NO SIGNAL (white) over MIC SILENT and dims the words to 55%. Each explains itself in the remarks line.
- **Finalizing:** no plate while the final is on time. Past 1 s after the stop tap (2.5 s in a mode with a cleanup pass, whose normal path includes it) the flap reads DELAYED (plain) over the real elapsed time in half-second steps (+1.0 S, with · BATCH when the saved audio is going through batch). A batch pass on time reads BATCH (slate) over SAVED AUDIO. No progress bar and no fake motion.
- **Outcomes:** the status flap flips to PASTED, RE-ROUTED (sub VIA BATCH or RAW TEXT), HELD, FAILED or CANCELLED in its tone. RAW TEXT means the cleanup pass failed, ran past its time, or returned text too far from the transcript's length, so the raw transcript pasted. Every outcome except PASTED and CANCELLED gets a remarks line with the reason. Cancelled words dim to 35% before the strip leaves. The strip leaves 0.2 s after PASTED and 0.25 s after CANCELLED, since the take is over and the strip should get out of the way. RE-ROUTED holds 1.7 s, HELD 2.4 s and FAILED 2.8 s, so their remarks can be read.
- **Status column:** empty flaps keep their space, so the words never shift when a status arrives.
- **Motion:** a character module flips in two parts: the old top half folds down over 90 ms (cubic-bezier(.5, 0, 1, .6), rotateX 0 to −90° at 60px perspective, darkening to 40% brightness) while the old bottom half holds for 150 ms and then cuts away in one step. A word plate or the status flap flips as a two-stage fold at 260px perspective: the old top half falls over 80 ms (cubic-bezier(.5, 0, 1, .6), to 45% brightness), then the new bottom half lands over 70 ms (cubic-bezier(0, .6, .4, 1), from rotateX 90° and 50% brightness), 150 ms in all. A collapsing word shrinks to zero width and fades over 190 ms, and the line slides over 200 ms, both on the board ease cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1). The strip leaves by fading and dropping 6px over 160 ms on the same ease; it never animates in.
- **Reduce Motion:** every flap lands instantly, a collapsing word simply disappears, and the line jumps instead of sliding; the state still changes. The lamp keeps following the level, since it changes brightness, not position.
- **Increase Contrast:** the strip and remarks line take the Contrast Edge, and the tail and dim inks brighten per Colors.
- **Sounds:** Sonar, chosen over a set of flap clicks. Four cues: a ping with a short echo on the mic's first real audio, a lower ping on the stop tap, two pings for held or failed, and a short tone on cancel. Nothing plays for pasted, re-routed, or mode switch. The files in `Resources/Sounds` are rendered from the page's recipes and play through the normal output at the main volume, because many people keep the alert volume at zero and system alert sounds would be silent.

### Named Rules
**The Locked to the Clock Rule.** A flap turns only when the thing it shows changes: a timer digit when a second of audio is captured, a clock digit on the minute, a word when the transcript revises it, the status when the pipeline's state does. Nothing flips for effect.

**The Stage Trace Rule.** Every change in Re-run traces to the stage that made it, by its tag in the text and its count on the line diagram. A change with no stage is a bug in the view.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep Mic-Live Amber (#ffb000) on the live lamp alone, lit only while real samples flow and brightened by the buffer level.
- **Do** paint every flap as two faces split by a 1px hairline at the exact midline.
- **Do** carry outcome by plate fill: PASTED plain, RE-ROUTED slate, HELD white, FAILED red, CANCELLED gray.
- **Do** keep the arriving words (24px, width 80, mixed case) the only large type on the indicator.
- **Do** use the Label style (700 10px, width 75, 0.12em, uppercase, Dim Enamel) for every column head and field label, and the same column names everywhere.
- **Do** set every number in tabular figures.
- **Do** make Reduce Motion land flaps instantly while the state still changes, and keep Increase Contrast's brighter rules, inks and #8a8680 edges.
- **Do** show the strip in one frame at key-down and post the destination first.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use amber for focus, selection, hover, a badge, a control, the wordmark, or any state other than the live mic.
- **Don't** mark a tone with a colored edge, side stripe or dot; tone lives in the plate's fill.
- **Don't** use the frosted bottom pill with a gradient waveform, or any frosted, blurred or vibrant material.
- **Don't** add glows beyond the level-driven lamp; static decorative glow is not part of this world.
- **Don't** show fake progress while finalizing; past the on-time window the flap reads DELAYED with the real elapsed time.
- **Don't** uppercase dictated words or set them in the condensed plate widths.
- **Don't** animate the strip in, or flip a module whose character did not change.
